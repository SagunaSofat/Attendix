What is Attendix?

Attendix is an online attendance management system designed to make attendance tracking simple, organized and easy to access. It provides students with a clear view of their attendance percentage, subject-wise records and attendance history.

🎯

Our Objective

The main objective of Attendix is to provide a simple and organized way to view and manage attendance information.

📊

Easy Tracking

Students can easily view their overall attendance, subject-wise percentage and attendance records in one place.

⚡

Simple Interface

Attendix uses a clean and simple interface so that attendance information can be understood quickly without unnecessary complexity.

📅

Organized Records

Attendance history can be displayed in an organized format, making it easier for students to keep track of their attendance.

About This Project

Attendix is a frontend-based academic project created to demonstrate the design of an online attendance management system using HTML and CSS. The project focuses on creating a simple, user-friendly and organized interface.
